About the Institute

Rooted in food science, grown into industry.

The State Institute of Food Processing Technology (SIFPT), Lucknow — formerly the Government Fruit Preservation and Canning Institute (GFPCI) — has trained generations of technologists who now lead in food processing, horticulture, dairy and public administration across India.

Our alumni association exists to keep that shared foundation alive: a place to find batchmates, share opportunities, mentor current students, and give back to the institute that started it all.

The First Grand Reunion

A milestone gathering that brought SIFPT's professional legacy back under one roof.

09 May 2026 · Lucknow

SIFPT's First Alumni Reunion Meet

Former students from across the country returned to campus for the institute's first large-scale alumni reunion, spanning careers in MSME, Khadi & Village Industries, Food Processing, Horticulture and Basic Education departments.

Senior alumni now at organisations like Domino's, Bisleri, Amul, Coca-Cola, PepsiCo, Britannia and Parle joined as guests of honour, alongside B.L. Meena, Additional Chief Secretary, Horticulture & Food Processing Department, as chief guest.

The meet closed with a proposal to formally register the alumni association — to support student placements, mentorship and long-term industry ties.
